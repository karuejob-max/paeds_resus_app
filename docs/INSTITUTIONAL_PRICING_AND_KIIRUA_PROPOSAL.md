## 1. Purpose

This document records the commercial decisions and proposal structure used for the St. Theresa Mission Hospital – Kiirua institutional opportunity. It exists so that the proposal, pricing logic, and future-agent guidance do not live only in a PDF, a chat session, or an execution log.

The institutional strategy is **ERS-first**: Paeds Resus sells measurable hospital emergency readiness, with AHA-aligned training as a supporting competency layer and the CPD Portal as a separate measurement and reporting layer. Do not reduce this offer to a bulk certificate-seat quote.

## 3. Kiirua commercial anchor

The Kiirua proposal uses the following planning assumptions, subject to HR verification and the signed statement of work:

| Component | Planning basis | Year-one price |
|---|---|---:|
| ILSP competency training | Approximately 200 staff at the approved automatic ILSP rate of KES 7,000 per staff member | **KES 1,400,000** |
| Institutional Emergency Readiness System activation | Facility-level price: Level 4 KES 200,000; Level 5 KES 350,000; Level 6 KES 600,000; Quaternary from KES 900,000 custom quote | **To be confirmed by verified facility level** |
| ICPD | Approximately 500 total staff at the 301–500 tier: KES 800 per staff per year | **KES 400,000** |
| **Year-one programme total** | Subject to HR-verified headcount, verified facility level, cohort scope, and final statement of work | **From KES 2,000,000 at Level 4; Level 5 planning total KES 2,150,000** |

The proposal uses a facility-level IERS price rather than a percentage surcharge. The IERS is a productised institutional capability, not a percentage add-on. The original KES 2,600,000 planning anchor assumed approximately 200 staff at KES 10,000 each plus KES 200,000 IERS and KES 400,000 ICPD; it must be recalculated using the approved ILSP KES 7,000 rate and Kiirua’s verified facility level.

## 4. CPD Portal volume pricing — canonical rule

**KES 800 per staff per year is the approved ICPD fee for facilities with 301–500 total staff.** It is not the default price for every institution and it is not a general discount that agents may apply outside the defined tier.

The CPD Portal is a hospital-wide measurement and reporting layer. It tracks attendance, participation, targets, evidence, and leadership reports across existing clinical and non-clinical CPD activity. The hospital owns the underlying CPD content and approves attendance; Paeds Resus provides the portal, tracking, visibility, and reporting layer.

| Verified total facility staff | CPD Portal price per staff per year | Commercial interpretation |
|---:|---:|---|
| 1–100 | **KES 1,000** | Standard small-facility rate |
| 101–300 | **KES 900** | Volume-discounted rate |
| 301–500 | **KES 800** | **Discounted Kiirua planning rate** |
| 501+ | **Negotiated individually** | Larger-facility or network pricing; do not assume KES 800 continues automatically |

### 4.1 Pricing rules agents must preserve

1. Use the **verified total facility staff headcount**, not only the clinical cohort, to select the CPD Portal tier.
2. A facility with exactly 500 staff is in the **301–500 tier** and therefore receives KES 800 per staff per year under this schedule.
3. Facilities with **501 or more staff** move to negotiated pricing; KES 800 does not automatically extend beyond 500 staff.
4. Do not call the CPD Portal a full learning-management system unless content creation, curriculum accreditation, assessment authoring, or broader LMS functionality is separately scoped and priced.
5. CPD Portal pricing is separate from BLS/ACLS/PALS/NRP training fees, IERS activation, official AHA fees, travel, venue, catering, content development, and other out-of-scope services.
6. The final invoice must use the HR-verified headcount and the signed statement of work. Proposal estimates are not a substitute for headcount confirmation.

### 4.2 CPD Portal examples

| Verified staff count | Applicable tier | Annual CPD Portal price |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 80 | 1–100 at KES 1,000 | KES 80,000 |
| 250 | 101–300 at KES 900 | KES 225,000 |
| 500 | 301–500 at KES 800 | KES 400,000 |

These examples are direct applications of the tier schedule; they are not separate discounts.

## 5. Certification and brand language

Use the following distinction in every proposal, quote, sales conversation, and learner-facing document:

- **Paeds Resus Institutional Competency Certificate:** issued by Paeds Resus under the agreed institutional competency pathway, after the agreed learning, assessment, and practical requirements are met. It must not be represented as an official AHA provider card.
- **Official AHA BLS/ACLS/PALS/other credential:** included only where the exact AHA course pathway, applicable requirements, and official fees are expressly contracted and delivered under the relevant AHA rules.

Use **Paeds Resus** for the organisation and institutional portal, **Paeds Resus Limited** for the legal training entity, and **ResusGPS** only for the bedside clinical guidance product. Do not call the institutional portal “ResusGPS.”

## 6. Scope and delivery guardrails

The institutional offer has three connected but separately defined components:

| Component | Purpose | Must be stated in the scope |
|---|---|---|
| ILSP competency training | Build a verified institutional cohort through learning, facilitated delivery, practical assessment, remediation where required, and Paeds Resus certification evidence | Cohort size, staff list, sessions, assessment, skills validation, certificate type, and any official AHA pathway |
| IERS activation | Create a distributed, role-defined, measurable emergency-response system | Verified facility level, baseline, unit map, UTL/ERTL rotation, response roles, event review, dashboard access, support, and review schedule |
| ICPD | Measure existing institutional CPD activity | Verified total staff, admin access, attendance/evidence capture, targets, reports, support, and exclusions |

The standard rollout is 0–15 days mobilisation and baseline, 16–45 days first cohort and readiness configuration, 46–75 days scale and simulation, and 76–90 days review and activation. Final dates depend on Kiirua or the client providing staff registers, nominated leads, approved schedules, clinical governance input, and access dependencies.

## 7. Commercial decision rules

The institutional programme should be sold on the value of **distributed readiness and measurable improvement**, not on the number of certificates alone. The price should not be reduced first. If a client cannot approve the full scope, offer a smaller, clearly bounded pilot that reduces units, cohort size, or delivery scope; do not discount the same obligations while leaving the delivery burden unchanged.

Every proposal must include:

- a decision requested;
- exact deliverables and acceptance evidence;
- a 30/60/90-day or equivalent rollout plan;
- process KPIs rather than unsupported mortality claims;
- named client and Paeds Resus responsibilities;
- data-governance principles;
- payment triggers;
- renewal and scope boundaries; and
- a final-agreement clarification checklist.
