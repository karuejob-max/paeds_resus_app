## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy

The **Institutional Emergency Readiness Management System™ (IERMS™)** is the canonical, evidence-based standard for hospital-wide emergency readiness with **paediatric priority framing**.

### The Child at Both Ends of the Loop

The IERMS™ is Paeds Resus's primary mechanism for closing the learning loop across health institutions:

> **On one end** is the child who died (*The Book of the Unforgotten* — capturing systemic failure patterns, honoring every life lost, recording where the system broke).  
> **On the other end** is the child who lives because the system learned.

This cycle can **never stop**. Eliminating preventable childhood deaths is an infinite commitment. We can never comfortably declare that all preventable deaths have been eliminated; therefore, every institution adopting IERMS™ becomes a node in a perpetual, self-correcting Adaptive Learning System.

### Whole-Hospital Emergency Response with Paediatric Priority

In low- and middle-income country (LMIC) health systems, specialized isolated paediatric hospitals rarely exist in regional or county referral networks. Paediatric care occurs within general hospitals where paediatric wards face high Patient:Nurse/Provider ratios. 

Preventable paediatric deaths rarely stem from lack of individual goodwill; they stem from **systemic execution gaps at the point of care**: delayed recognition of decompensation, fragmented activation pathways, absence of bedside decision support, unstocked emergency supplies, and lack of blameless near-miss feedback loops (the *Know-Do Gap*).

IERMS™ addresses this reality by **mobilizing the entire hospital emergency infrastructure** (a 24/7 nurse-led Emergency Response Team) with paediatric priority. This whole-hospital mobilization protects children while simultaneously elevating emergency care for all acute patient populations across the facility.

### Domain 1: Governance, Policy & 24/7 Nurse-Led ERT Activation

- **1.1 Institutional Governance Hierarchy:** The facility must establish a 6-tier emergency governance framework:
  1. **Hospital Executive:** Executive Sponsor & Legal Sign-Off (CEO / Medical Director / CNO).
  2. **Emergency Readiness Committee (ERC):** Multi-disciplinary steering committee (Medical, Nursing, Quality, Pharmacy, Engineering).
  3. **Emergency Readiness Coordinators (ERCs):** Designated operational leads overseeing hospital-wide readiness day-to-day.
  4. **Unit Team Leaders (UTLs):** Ward/Unit nursing leads responsible for shift rosters and daily equipment audits.
  5. **Emergency Response Team (ERT):** Multi-disciplinary responders for Code Blue/Yellow activations.
  6. **Emergency Response Team Leader (ERTL):** Scene commander during an active code response.
- **1.2 The 5-Stage Resuscitation Chain:** All emergency workflows must enforce the 5-stage clinical continuum:
  $$\text{Recognition} \longrightarrow \text{Activation} \longrightarrow \text{Response} \longrightarrow \text{Stabilization} \longrightarrow \text{Recovery}$$
- **1.3 Real-World Staffing & Whole-Hospital Reciprocity:** Acknowledging the African referral baseline (~2 nurses/shift), the ERT protocol enforces **Whole-Hospital Reciprocity** ("scratch my back, I'll scratch yours"). Paeds ward nurses and adult ER/ward nurses form a shared ERT network—responding mutually to Code Blues across adult, maternity, and paediatric units.
- **1.4 24/7 Activation Roster:** Every shift must publish designated ERTL and ERT Primary Responders on the central roster.
- **1.5 Time-to-Response Target:** Code Blue activation to first qualified responder at bedside within **< 3 minutes** hospital-wide.

---

### Domain 2: Point-of-Care Bedside Guidance (ResusGPS Deployment)

- **2.1 Mobile & Tablet Availability:** ResusGPS must be deployed on dedicated ward tablets or verified point-of-care mobile devices in ER, Paediatric Ward, NBU/NICU, ICU, Maternity, and General Surgical Wards.
- **2.2 Bedside Utilization Standard:** ResusGPS must be used for active guidance during paediatric and adult resuscitation & critical stabilization:
  - Weight-based drug & fluid volume calculator (preventing 10x dosing errors).
  - Visual ABCDE structured assessment & stabilization flow.
  - Interactive visual CPR Clock (2-minute cycle prompts, epinephrine timer).
- **2.3 Offline Reliability:** Bedside devices must run PWA offline-capable software to ensure guidance is uninterrupted during network downtime.

---

### Domain 3: Safety Culture, Near-Miss Intelligence & Care Signal QI

- **3.1 Blameless Incident Reporting:** Facility leadership must enforce a non-punitive safety reporting culture utilizing **Care Signal**.
- **3.2 Monthly QI Cadence:** Facility Quality Improvement (QI) Committee must meet monthly to review Care Signal submissions, near-miss trends, and resuscitation debriefs.
- **3.3 Failure Pattern Knowledge Base (FPKB) Resolution:** Reported system gaps (e.g., missing paediatric bag-valve-mask in Ward B, delayed oxygen delivery) must be assigned owner action items and closed within 30 days.
- **3.4 Institutional Privacy Compliance:** All Care Signal reports processed for analytical rollups read strictly redacted derivatives in compliance with `PRIVACY_POLICY_FULL.md`.

---

### Domain 4: Clinical Workforce Competency & AHA-Aligned Training Mesh

- **4.1 Family-Centered Training & ACLS Economics:** Paeds Resus is a **Family-Centered Organization**. Training 100 RNs in ACLS provides a hospital-wide emergency baseline with superior unit economics and broad adult/maternity coverage, supported by targeted PALS certification for high-risk paediatric and NBU units.
- **4.2 Role-Mapped Training Distribution:** Training is targeted by clinical role:
  - **Paediatric Emergency & NBU/NICU Nurses:** PALS + NRP + Micro-courses.
  - **Adult/General Ward & ER Nurses:** BLS + ACLS + Paediatric Priority Modules.
  - **Medical Officers & Emergency Physicians:** ACLS + PALS.
  - **Support & Triage Staff:** High-Quality BLS.
- **4.3 Prerequisite & Pace Governance:** Strict compliance with platform gates:
  - BLS completion prerequisite before ACLS/PALS hands-on sign-off.
  - Nurse instalment pace rules (KES 2,500/month) respected without artificial lockouts.
- **4.4 Institutional Instructor Pathway:** Facility develops internal instructor capacity through the Paeds Resus 3-tier instructor pipeline (Provisional → Qualified → Lead Instructor) with formal mentor confirmation.

---

### Domain 5: Physical Readiness, Crash Cart Sizing & Equipment Audits

- **5.1 Standardized Crash Cart Layout:** Emergency trolleys across all units must maintain standardized layout and colour-coded Broselow/weight-based organisation.
- **5.2 Paediatric-Specific Sizing Audit:** Mandatory 100% availability of:
  - Uncuffed and cuffed endotracheal tubes (sizes 2.5 through 6.5).
  - Paediatric and infant bag-valve-masks with appropriate reservoir systems.
  - Intraosseous (IO) needles (15mm, 25mm) and infusion supplies.
  - Paediatric defibrillator pads and attenuator cables.
  - Paediatric suction catheters and size-appropriate oral airways (Guedel 00 to 3).
- **5.3 Audit Cadence:** Daily crash cart seal/checklist verification by shift in-charge; monthly comprehensive equipment audit uploaded to the Institutional Dashboard.

## 4. IERMS™ Accreditation Tiers

Facility readiness is evaluated using the [IERMS_AUDIT_SCORECARD_V1.md](./IERMS_AUDIT_SCORECARD_V1.md):

| Tier | Score Range | Status & Meaning | Renewal / Cadence |
|------|-------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Level 1** | `< 50 pts` | **Unprepared / High Risk** — Critical system gaps identified. Remediation plan required immediately. | Re-audit in 30 Days |
| **Level 2** | `50 – 69 pts` | **Baseline Functional** — Basic ERT roster & training active. Gaps exist in bedside guidance or equipment. | Re-audit in 90 Days |
| **Level 3** | `70 – 89 pts` | **IERMS™ Certified** — Fully functional 24/7 ERT, ResusGPS active, Care Signal QI live, >75% staff certified. | **Annual Accreditation** |
| **Level 4** | `90 – 100 pts` | **IERMS™ High-Reliability Exemplar** — Outstanding readiness, closed FPKB loops, internal lead instructors. | **Annual Accreditation** |

---

## 5. Governance & Re-Accreditation Cycle

1. **Initial Certification:** Granted upon achieving Level 3 or Level 4 score on the formal IERMS™ Audit Scorecard.
2. **Annual Review:** Required every 12 months. Requires submission of updated equipment audit, 12-month Care Signal QI action log, and verified staff training coverage.
3. **Continuous Data Validation:** Monthly institutional dashboard analytics (activations, time to response, training coverage) provide ongoing operational signals between annual formal audits.
