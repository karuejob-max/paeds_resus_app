## 1. Post-Event Debriefing Protocol (GAS Model)

Every Code Blue/Yellow resuscitation must conclude with a 5-minute **GAS** debriefing:
1. **G (Gather):** Bring team together; review objective facts of event.
2. **A (Analyze):** What went well? What system gaps or delays occurred?
3. **S (Summarize):** Identify key learning points; log near-misses on Care Signal.

---

## 2. Non-Punitive Safety Commitment

- Debriefings and Care Signal reports focus strictly on **system improvement**, never personal blame.
- Fear of punitive action leads to hidden errors. Psychological safety ensures honest reporting so the system can learn and protect the next child.
