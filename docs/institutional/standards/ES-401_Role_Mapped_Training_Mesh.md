## 1. Role-Mapped Training Distribution

Facilities do not purchase generic seat bundles. Training is mapped to clinical roles:
- **Paediatric & NBU/NICU Nurses:** PALS + NRP + Micro-courses.
- **Adult & Emergency Nurses:** BLS + ACLS + Paediatric Priority Modules.
- **Medical Officers:** ACLS + PALS.
- **Support Staff:** BLS.

---

## 2. Prerequisite & Pace Governance

1. **BLS Prerequisite:** Must complete BLS practical sign-off before receiving ACLS or PALS practical certification.
2. **Nurse Instalment Pace:** Enforce KES 2,500/month pace rules fairly without artificial lockouts.
