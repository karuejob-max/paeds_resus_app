## 1. Scope & Golden Hour Objectives

Standardized resuscitation for newborns in Labour Ward, Operating Theatre, and NBU/NICU.

---

## 2. Initial Neonatal Resuscitation Algorithm (First 60 Seconds)

1. **Warm, Dry, Position, Stimulate:** Thermal protection, clear airway if obstructed.
2. **Evaluate Breathing & Heart Rate:**
   - If apnoeic/gasping or $\text{HR} < 100\text{ bpm}$: Start Positive Pressure Ventilation (PPV) with room air or 21% $\text{O}_2$.
   - Monitor pulse oximetry on right hand/wrist (pre-ductal).
3. **If $\text{HR} < 60\text{ bpm}$ after 30s effective PPV:**
   - Increase $\text{FiO}_2$ to 100%, secure airway, initiate chest compressions (3:1 ratio, 90 compressions + 30 breaths/min).
