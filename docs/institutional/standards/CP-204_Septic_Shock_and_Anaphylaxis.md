## 1. Paediatric Septic Shock First-Hour Bundle

1. **Recognize:** Altered perfusion, fever/hypothermia, tachycardia, delayed capillary refill.
2. **Access & Fluid:** IV/IO within 5 minutes; $10\text{–}20\text{ mL/kg}$ fluid boluses over 10–20 mins (reassess after each bolus for rales/hepatomegaly).
3. **Antibiotics:** First dose IV/IO broad-spectrum antibiotics within **60 minutes**.
4. **Vasoactive Support:** Epinephrine infusion ($0.05\text{–}0.3\text{ mcg/kg/min}$) if fluid-refractory.

---

## 2. Acute Anaphylaxis Protocol

1. **IM Epinephrine Immediately:** $0.01\text{ mg/kg}$ of 1:1,000 solution ($1\text{ mg/mL}$) into anterolateral mid-thigh.
2. **Airway & Oxygen:** High-flow $\text{O}_2$, prepare for difficult airway if stridor present.
3. **IV Fluid & Adjuncts:** Isotonic fluid bolus for hypotension; IV antihistamines & corticosteroids second-line.
