## 1. The 3 Instructor Tiers

1. **Provisional Instructor:** Completed Instructor Course, paired with a named mentor.
2. **Qualified Instructor:** Mentor confirms 3 independently-led training groups.
3. **Lead Instructor:** Mentored 10 different mentees to Qualified status.

---

## 2. Quality & Competency Governance

Instructor credentialing requires per-course provider competency (must hold active provider credential in specific course taught).
