# QS-502: Monthly QI Committee & FPKB System Gap Closure Standard

> **Standard Code:** QS-502  
> **Series:** Quality System Standards (QS)  
> **Status:** Official Standard v1.0 — July 2026  
> **Governance:** Paeds Resus Clinical Stewardship Committee  
> **Related Documents:** [QS-501_Care_Signal_Incident_Reporting.md](./QS-501_Care_Signal_Incident_Reporting.md)

---

## 1. Monthly QI Meeting Cadence

Hospital QI Committee meets monthly to review Care Signal submissions, near-miss trends, and resuscitation debrief logs.

---

## 2. FPKB Action Item Resolution

Identified system gaps (e.g. unstocked paediatric airway box, delayed blood transfusion) must be assigned owner action items and closed within **30 days**.
