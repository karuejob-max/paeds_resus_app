## 1. System Philosophy & Purpose

The **Institutional Emergency Readiness Management System™ (IERMS™)** architecture establishes a high-reliability, self-correcting clinical ecosystem across healthcare facilities.

### The Perpetual Learning Loop

Everything built in IERMS™ connects the child at both ends of the loop:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                              THE PERPETUAL LEARNING LOOP                               │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                                        │
│   [ The Child Who Died ]                                    [ The Child Who Lives ]    │
│  (The Book of the Unforgotten)                              (The Next Patient Saved)   │
│             │                                                          ▲               │
│             ▼                                                          │               │
│   Care Signal Incident  ──────► FPKB Pattern  ──────► Updated ResusGPS │               │
│   Near-Miss Report              Discovery             & Target Mesh ───┘               │
│                                                                                        │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **The Origin:** *The Book of the Unforgotten* records where the system broke—honoring every life lost by capturing the underlying failure pattern.
2. **The Transformation:** Incident reports (Care Signal) and bedside cases (ResusGPS) are processed into the Failure Pattern Knowledge Base (FPKB).
3. **The Outcome:** Updated bedside decision guidance, targeted micro-courses, and physical equipment fixes ensure the next child lives.

## 2. Whole-Hospital Mobilization Baseline

In low- and middle-income country (LMIC) health systems, regional referral hospitals operate with **~2 nurses per department per shift**. Paediatric wards experience high Patient:Nurse ratios.

Rather than fragmenting resources into unstaffable specialty codes (e.g. separate stroke or behavioral teams), IERMS™ establishes **whole-hospital ERT mobilization**:
- One coordinated 24/7 Nurse-Led ERT responds across departments.
- Primary ward nurse stays at bedside while cross-unit responders mobilize.
- Paediatric priority framing ensures time-critical paediatric arrest and pre-arrest decompensation receive immediate multi-disciplinary escalation.
