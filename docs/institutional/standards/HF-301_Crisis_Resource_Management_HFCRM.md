## 1. Core Principles of Resuscitation CRM

1. **Clear Team Leadership:** Designate one clear Team Leader who steps back from physical procedures to maintain global situational awareness.
2. **Role Assignment:** Explicit role distribution (Compressor, Airway/Ventilation, Medication/Dosing, Recorder/CPR Clock).
3. **Know Your Limitations:** Any team member must call for help early when overloaded or uncertain.
