## 1. Purpose & Triggers

Code Yellow is activated to prevent cardiac arrest by intervening early during physiological decompensation:
- Severe respiratory distress (grunting, severe chest indrawing, $\text{SpO}_2 < 90\%$).
- Decompensated shock (capillary refill $> 3\text{s}$, weak pulses, altered mental status).
- Sudden drop in AVPU consciousness score.

---

## 2. Bedside ABCDE Intervention

1. **Airway & Breathing:** High-flow oxygen, position airway, check bag-valve-mask sizing.
2. **Circulation:** Rapid IV/IO access, ResusGPS fluid bolus calculation ($10\text{–}20\text{ mL/kg}$ isotonic crystalloid over 10–20 mins).
3. **Disability:** Rapid blood glucose check, treat hypoglycemia ($2\text{ mL/kg}$ 10% Dextrose).
4. **Exposure:** Maintain normothermia, inspect for rash/purpura.
