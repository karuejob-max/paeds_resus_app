## 1. Reporting Workflow & Purpose

Staff log near-misses and system failures on Care Signal (equipment failure, medication delay, oxygen unavailability).

---

## 2. Privacy & Data Pipeline

All reports are processed through privacy redaction cron jobs (`care-signal-redact.ts`) before analytical aggregation in compliance with `PRIVACY_POLICY_FULL.md`.
