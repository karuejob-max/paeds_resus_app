## 8. Decline, replacement, and absence protocol

The facility should answer these questions before publishing a roster:

1. How far in advance should a provider accept or decline a duty?
2. Which decline reasons require immediate escalation?
3. Who receives a UTL decline notification, and through which route?
4. Who confirms the replacement provider?
5. What happens when the replacement also declines?
6. How are resignation, redeployment, sick leave, leave, and connectivity loss handled?
7. Who confirms that the replacement actually accepted the dated duty?
8. Which shifts remain uncovered until resolved, and where are they displayed?
9. Who can pause a roster or change a team during an active shift?
10. How are urgent role changes communicated beyond the app?

## 9. Activation and reporting boundaries

- Use the facility’s emergency activation route first when a life-threatening event occurs.
- Use IERS activation and team tools according to local policy and the current platform workflow.
- Use ResusGPS as structured reference support for trained providers; it does not replace judgement, senior review, local protocol, or emergency services.
- Use a named targeted ERT role report for factual role-at-event observations linked to an activation snapshot.
- Use anonymous Care/Code Signal for provider QI observations, including events observed by providers outside the ERT.
- Never enter patient names, hospital numbers, phone numbers, photographs, or unnecessary identifying details in IERS improvement reports or drills.
- Follow the facility’s official clinical incident, safeguarding, statutory, and medical-record pathways separately.
