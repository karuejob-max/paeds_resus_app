# ILSP, IERS, and ICPD Marketing and Pricing Update

**Status:** Approved implementation and marketing source of truth  
**Scope:** Institutional Life Support Program (ILSP), Institutional Emergency Readiness System (IERS), and Institutional Continuous Professional Development (ICPD)

## ILSP — Institutional Life Support Program

ILSP is a managed, institution-paid facility cohort for life-support learning, delivery, readiness coordination, practical assessment, and completion evidence, scoped around the institution’s staff list, departments, and schedule.

The published institutional rate is **KES 7,000 per staff member**. This is an automatic 30% reduction from the KES 10,000 list price and applies to every ILSP institutional checkout. It is not a manually granted one-off discount. A Global Admin full-waiver entitlement may still reduce an order to zero; percentage entitlements must not stack with the automatic 30% rate.

Use the following client-facing description:

> **ILSP — Institutional Life Support Program.** A managed, institution-paid facility cohort for life-support learning, delivery, practical assessment, and completion evidence, scoped around your staff list, departments, and schedule. Priced at **KES 7,000 per staff member**. Institutions can absorb the cost or recover it through an approved arrangement such as payroll deduction. Contact us to plan your institutional cohort.

The catalog price field is **KES 7,000 per staff member**. The certificate boundary remains a Paeds Resus certificate unless a separate official AHA pathway is contracted and delivered under the applicable requirements.

## IERS — Institutional Emergency Readiness System

IERS is a hospital-wide operating layer for emergency response: team activation, roster and role clarity, equipment readiness, drills, evidence review, corrective actions, leadership reporting, and ResusGPS bedside guidance.

IERS is priced by the facility classification already captured during institutional onboarding:

| Facility level | Annual IERS planning price |
|---|---:|
| Level 4 — Primary care / sub-county hospital | KES 200,000 |
| Level 5 — Secondary care / county referral | KES 350,000 |
| Level 6 — Tertiary / national referral | KES 600,000 |
| Quaternary / highly specialised | From KES 900,000; custom quote |

These are first-pass planning anchors and may be revisited with client evidence. The final quote must use the verified facility level. Use **“from KES 200,000/year, tiered by facility level”** in public catalog copy.

## ICPD — Institutional Continuous Professional Development

ICPD is the institutional service for organising, verifying, tracking, and reporting staff professional development across departments. It covers scheduled sessions, verified attendance, targets, certificates, and leadership reporting. It is not a full content-authoring LMS unless separately scoped.

Pricing is based on verified total facility staff:

| Verified total facility staff | Annual price per staff member |
|---:|---:|
| 1–100 | KES 1,000 |
| 101–300 | KES 900 |
| 301–500 | KES 800 |
| 501+ | Custom quote |

Use **“from KES 800/staff/year”** in catalog copy, with the final quote based on verified staff numbers.

## Safe commercial presentation

The three products are connected but separate. ILSP builds a defined life-support cohort. IERS builds the hospital’s emergency-readiness operating system. ICPD measures and reports professional-development activity. None should be represented as a guarantee of clinical outcomes, legal immunity, official AHA certification, or bedside competence beyond the evidence and pathway actually delivered.

For Kiirua, recalculate the existing planning proposal using the approved ILSP rate and verified IERS facility level. The previous KES 2,600,000 anchor assumed approximately 200 providers at KES 10,000 each, KES 200,000 IERS, and KES 400,000 ICPD. At approximately 200 ILSP providers, the training component becomes **KES 1,400,000**. The programme therefore starts at **KES 2,000,000 for a Level 4 planning case**, becomes approximately **KES 2,150,000 for Level 5**, and approximately **KES 2,400,000 for Level 6**, before travel, venue, official AHA fees, or other separately scoped items.

All final amounts remain subject to HR-verified headcount, facility classification, cohort scope, training pathway, and a signed statement of work.
